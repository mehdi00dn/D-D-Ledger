Privacy

What Campaign Ledger keeps about you, why, and what you can do about it. Plain words, no tracking.

What we keep

  • Your account: username, email address, and a scrambled (hashed) password. If you use Google sign-in, Google's id for you instead of a password.
  • Your profile, if you fill it in: display name, bio, photo, and who may see your profile. You choose each of these.
  • What you make: campaigns, characters, maps, notes, treasury entries, dice rolls and uploaded images. Other people at your table can see what you put in a shared campaign.

We never ask for your real name, phone number, birth date or address.

Who sees it

Your email address is only ever shown to you. Your profile follows the visibility you picked: everyone signed in, people you play with, or only you. Nothing is public to people who are not signed in.

What we do not do

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking scripts, no third-party fonts. We do not sell or share your data.

IP addresses

We do not store them. To slow down password guessing, the server keeps a scrambled (hashed) form of an address for 24 hours, then deletes it.

Cookies and storage

One session cookie keeps you signed in (up to 30 days with "Remember me", otherwise until you close the browser). Your browser also remembers a few settings on your own device, such as theme and whether a tip was shown. Neither is used to follow you.

Services that handle your data for us

  • Supabase stores the database and uploaded files.
  • Vercel runs the site and keeps its request logs.
  • Google, only if you choose Google sign-in.
  • An email service sends confirmation and password-reset mail.

Your choices

  • Download everything we hold about you, and change your profile and visibility, in Account settings.
  • Delete your account there. It removes your account and the characters you keep in Your characters. A campaign you alone own is handed to another member or deleted, as you choose.
