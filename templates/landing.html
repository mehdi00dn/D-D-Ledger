01 / Keep the story organized

Every campaign has a story. Keep yours together.

Bring the moving parts of your campaign into one organized space. Keep track of your party, important people, notes, quests, and the details that make your world feel alive.

  • Organize campaigns and invite your players.
  • Keep characters, factions, and important notes connected.
  • Track campaign details without losing sight of the bigger picture.

02 / Bring characters to life

More than a character sheet.

Your characters are at the heart of every adventure. Keep their details, abilities, stats, and stories together, so the information you need is always close at hand.

  • Create and manage player characters and NPCs.
  • Keep character details and important notes organized.
  • Give every character a place in your campaign.

03 / Keep the action moving

Less time managing the encounter. More time in the adventure.

Keep combat organized while the action unfolds. Follow initiative, manage hit points, track conditions, and keep the encounter moving without losing the flow of the session.

  • Track initiative, turns, and rounds.
  • Manage hit points, damage, healing, and conditions.
  • Keep encounter information available to the people who need it.
{#- IMG-05: battle tracker: initiative order and health controls -#}

04 / Explore your world

Give your adventures a place to unfold.

Bring locations and encounters to the table with interactive maps. Place tokens, mark important areas, measure distances, and reveal the world as your players explore.

{#- IMG-06: map editor with grid, tokens and fog of war -#}
  • Use maps as a shared space for exploration and encounters.
  • Place and manage tokens.
  • Use grids, measurements, and drawing tools.
  • Control what players can see with fog of war and visibility options.
