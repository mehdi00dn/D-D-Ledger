{{ icon('info') }} How encounters work
  1. {{ icon('skull') }}
    Pick monsters. Create an encounter, search the 2024 monster manual and add the creatures you want. Use + and − for how many.
  2. {{ icon('shield') }}
    Check the difficulty. Every monster is worth XP. The meter compares the total with what your party can take: Low is an easy fight, Moderate a fair one, High a hard one, and Over High may be deadly.
  3. {{ icon('swords') }}
    Run it. "Run in battle" adds all the monsters to the battle tracker. Monsters you have used before are reused, so your character list does not fill up with copies.
