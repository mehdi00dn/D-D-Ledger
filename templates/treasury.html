{{ icon('info') }} How the treasury works
  1. {{ icon('coins') }}
    Purses. Coins always sit in a purse: the shared party purse (the loot nobody owns yet) or a character's own purse. Each purse is just the total of its entries below.
  2. {{ icon('plus') }}
    Coins in, coins out. Record loot, pay, shopping or a bribe in "Add or spend". Spending makes change for you: pay 1 gp from a platinum piece and 9 gp come back.
  3. {{ icon('swap') }}
    Share or hand over. Split loot evenly between characters, give coins to one friend, or put them back into the party purse in "Share or hand over". Whatever does not divide stays where it was.

Coins: 1 cp copper · 1 sp silver = 10 cp · 1 ep electrum = 5 sp · 1 gp gold = 10 sp · 1 pp platinum = 10 gp. Only the DM and whoever made or plays a character can spend from that character's purse.
