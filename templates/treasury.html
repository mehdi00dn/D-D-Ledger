{{ icon('info') }} How the treasury works
  1. {{ icon('coins') }}
    Purses. Coins always sit in a purse: the shared party purse (loot nobody owns yet) or a character's own purse. A purse is the total of its entries below.
  2. {{ icon('plus') }}
    Coins in, coins out. Record loot, pay, shopping or a bribe with "Add or spend", from the page or from a purse's own button. Spending makes change for you.
  3. {{ icon('swap') }}
    Share or hand over. Split coins evenly between purses or give them to one. Whatever does not divide stays where it was.

Coins: 1 cp copper · 1 sp silver = 10 cp · 1 ep electrum = 5 sp · 1 gp gold = 10 sp · 1 pp platinum = 10 gp. The gold figure next to a purse is everything in it counted as gold, so 40 gp and 15 pp show as 190 gp. Only the DM and whoever made or plays a character can spend from that character's purse.
