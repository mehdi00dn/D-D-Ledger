About Campaign Ledger

A free tool for people who run and play tabletop role-playing games, built to keep everything a campaign needs in one place.

What you can do

  • Characters and factions: sheets with abilities, saves, skills and attacks, portraits, and your own characters you can bring to any campaign.
  • Battle: initiative, turns, hit points, conditions, death saves, and short and long rests.
  • Maps: upload or draw a map, place tokens, draw shapes, measure distances and reveal it with fog of war.
  • Quests, notes and the party treasury: prepare before the session and keep the record after it.
  • Dice and a rules reference: roll with the group watching, and look up spells, monsters and items.

Who it is for

A Dungeon Master gets the tools to run a session. Players see only what they should, and everyone at the table sees changes as they happen.

Where it stands

Campaign Ledger is free while it is being tested and is built and looked after by one person. It uses the open D&D 5e System Reference Document for its rules reference. Feedback is welcome on the contact page.
